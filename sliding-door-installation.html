The Job

Why Sliding Door Installation Is Its Own Skill

Sliding and patio doors carry more weight and see more daily use than a standard entry door, which means the margin for error is smaller. A track that's even slightly out of level leads to dragging, sticking, or a door that won't seal — problems that show up months after a rushed install, not on day one.

KO Carpentry installs both ProVia and Alliance Hawthorne patio doors, matched to your home's opening and your preference for glass configuration and finish. Every install is set level and plumb, with proper flashing and sealing so the door operates smoothly and keeps weather out of your home.

Warning Signs

Signs It's Time to Replace Your Patio Door

Hard to slideDragging, sticking, or needing a shove to close
Won't seal fullyGaps at the track letting in air or water
Foggy glassSeal failure between panes on double-glazed units
Visible frame damageWarping, rot, or corrosion around the track
