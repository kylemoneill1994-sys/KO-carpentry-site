Door Installation for Blue Springs Homes

A door installation is only as good as the frame it's set in. Out-of-square openings, poor shimming, or skipped flashing are why so many "new" doors still let in drafts or stick within a year. That matters across Blue Springs's mix of housing — older homes with settled framing and newer builds alike.

KO Carpentry installs ProVia entry doors, known for solid construction and weather performance. Every install includes proper leveling, shimming, sill pan flashing, and sealing around the frame.
