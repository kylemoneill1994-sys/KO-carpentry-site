Window Replacement Built for Grain Valley Homes

Grain Valley has a real mix of housing stock — established neighborhoods with original single-pane windows from decades back, alongside newer construction that still benefits from an upgrade to better-performing glass. Whichever category your home falls into, window replacement means keeping your existing frame and opening intact: the old sash comes out, a new insert window goes in, with no drywall or siding disruption.

KO Carpentry installs Alliance Hawthorne replacement windows, built to handle Missouri's full range of weather — humid summers, cold winters, and everything in between. Every install includes proper flashing, insulation, and sealing so you actually feel the difference in comfort and your energy bill.
