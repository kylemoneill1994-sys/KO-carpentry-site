Sliding Door Installation for Oak Grove Homes

Sliding and patio doors carry more weight and see more daily use than a standard entry door, which means the margin for error is smaller. A track that's even slightly out of level leads to dragging, sticking, or a door that won't seal.

KO Carpentry installs both ProVia and Alliance Hawthorne patio doors, matched to your home's opening and your preference for glass configuration and finish. Every install is set level and plumb, with proper flashing and sealing.
